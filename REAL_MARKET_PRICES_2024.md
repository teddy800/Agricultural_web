## Economic Context (2024)

### Currency Impact
- July 2024: Ethiopian Birr devalued from 57 ETB/USD to 112-114 ETB/USD
- Result: 100% devaluation in 10 days
- Impact: Significant upward pressure on all commodity prices
- Inflation: Ongoing high inflation affecting food prices

### Market Dynamics
- Urban population growth increasing demand
- Supply chain challenges
- Export market opportunities (especially coffee)
- Government food security policies
